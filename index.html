Deal Check

Know the deal before you make the offer.

Enter a US address. In about a minute you get the public record, what comparable homes rent for, and a graded score for the neighbourhood, next to the map.

The record

Beds, baths, living area, lot, year built and last sale, from public records.

The rent

Median rent from nearby listings, sized to the property and adjustable.

The neighbourhood

A score out of 100 and a letter grade from ten weighted factors, with sources.

Information only. Not an appraisal, legal or financial advice. Privacy and Terms.
