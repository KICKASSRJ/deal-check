Who else processes your address

  • RentCast supplies public property records.
  • Rentometer supplies rent comparisons.
  • Google supplies maps, geocoding, address suggestions, and (with Gemini and Google Search) the neighbourhood research.
  • Cloudflare hosts our server component. GitHub hosts this website.

Each provider handles the data under its own terms. We send them the address (and, for rent, the bedroom, bathroom and size filters you chose) and nothing that identifies you personally.

Your choices

  • Turn history off, download everything this device holds, or delete it all, any time, from Privacy in the app.
  • Clearing your browser’s site data also removes everything stored on your device.
  • You can deny or revoke location permission in your browser settings.

Security

All traffic uses HTTPS. API keys are held on the server and are never placed in the web page. The server accepts requests only from this website, validates every input and limits how often one visitor can ask. No system is perfectly secure, so please do not enter sensitive personal information into the address box.

Children

Deal Check is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Your rights

Because we hold no account data about you, most requests can be handled with the controls above. Depending on where you live (for example California, or the EU and UK), you may also have rights to access, correct or delete personal data and to complain to a regulator. Contact us at the address above and we will respond within the time the law requires.

Changes

If we change this policy in a meaningful way, we will update the date above and show a notice in the app.
